THE STORY BEGINS

A young man named Peter Parker was bitten by a radioactive spider during a school field trip. This life-changing event transformed him into the spectacular Spider-Man! With great power comes great responsibility, and Peter has dedicated his life to protecting New York City from the forces of evil.
